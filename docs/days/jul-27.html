😴 Jet Lag Strategy
Arrival Day Sleep Plan — Survive Tonight
You land at 16:05 KST but your body clock reads midnight Vancouver time. The key is to hold out until a reasonable Korean bedtime without crashing early. One bad nap now can knock your sleep schedule off for days.
16:05–18:00 KST
00:05–02:00 PDT
⚠️ Do NOT nap — even lying down "just for 20 min" will become 3 hours. Get the SIM, take the bus, check in, and go straight outside. Movement helps.
18:00–21:00 KST
02:00–05:00 PDT
Stay busy — Bright lights, food, and walking are your best friend right now. Eat a proper dinner even if you're not hungry.
21:00–22:00 KST
05:00–06:00 PDT
😴 Bedtime — this is your target. Take a melatonin (0.5–1mg), shower, black out the room. Don't fight it past 22:00 — earlier is fine.
07:00–08:00 KST
15:00–16:00 PDT
Wake up — set an alarm. Your body clock will likely wake you earlier (Vancouver afternoon = Seoul early morning). Get up, open curtains, get sunlight. Day 2 starts fresh. ☀️
☀️ Sunlight is the strongest reset signal — get outside in the morning on Day 2 (Jul 28). Breakfast on the hotel terrace or a walk before the Gangnam day helps lock in your new rhythm.
