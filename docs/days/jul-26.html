Before You Leave — Pre-Departure Checklist
Jul 23
📋 3 Days Before
Fill Out Korea e-Arrival Card
Korean immigration requires an electronic arrival card submitted up to 3 days before arrival. Complete it at e-arrivalcard.go.kr. Have your passport, flight number, and accommodation address (Shilla Stay Seodaemun) ready. Saves significant time at immigration on arrival.
🌐 e-arrivalcard.go.kr
Jul 24–25
✈️ 24–48 hrs Before
Online Check-In
Check in online with your airline to choose seats and download boarding passes. Most airlines open check-in 24 hours before departure. Having mobile boarding passes avoids a queue at the airport.

😴 Jet Lag Strategy
In-Flight Sleep Plan — Reset to KST
Vancouver → Seoul crosses 16 time zones eastward — the tougher direction. The goal is to land at 16:05 KST feeling functional, then push through to 21:00 KST bedtime. Follow this flight plan:
13:00–15:00 PDT
05:00–07:00 KST
Stay awake — it's afternoon for your body. Eat the meal service, watch a movie, get settled.
15:00–15:30 PDT
07:00–07:30 KST
Wind down — take melatonin (0.5–1mg low dose works best), put on eye mask and neck pillow, recline seat.
15:30–22:00 PDT
07:30–14:00 KST
😴 SLEEP — aim for ~6 hours. This is the critical window. Even if it feels unnatural (your body says it's afternoon), use the eye mask, earplugs, and blanket. Avoid checking the time.
22:00–00:05 PDT
14:00–16:05 KST
Wake up — ~2 hrs before landing. Freshen up, eat the pre-landing meal, open the window shade, get into Seoul headspace. ☀️
16:05 KST
00:05 PDT
Land in Seoul — your body thinks it's midnight, but it's 4pm in Korea. The plan from here is on the Jul 27 page. 🇰🇷
💊 Melatonin tip: low dose (0.5mg) taken 30 min before your target sleep time works better than the standard 5mg pill. Available at Shoppers Drug Mart before the flight.
Tip: T-money transit card comes free with your LG U+ SIM — pick up the SIM at Gate A, T1 Arrivals right after landing.
